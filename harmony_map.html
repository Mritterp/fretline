Key: the circle of fifths

The map is laid out according to the circle of fifths with Major key chords outside and minor key chords inside. From there each chord is broken into its harmonic Function (Tonic, Subdominant, and Dominant chords) and split into three sections.

The small circle pairs around the main chords repeat that same layout. Each one is a miniature map for a single target chord: its left circle is that target’s own subdominant side (its ii), and its right circle is its own dominant side (its V7), with the same supporting chords in the same places: the cadential 6/4 and sus4 chords that lead into the V7, the Neapolitan sixth and the diminished 7th. So V7/V does for G what the main V7 does for C, and ii7/V is the subdominant that leads to it. Medium shows only the right-hand (dominant) circles; Advanced adds the left-hand ones.

As you click a chord you will see suggested movements based on music theory.
